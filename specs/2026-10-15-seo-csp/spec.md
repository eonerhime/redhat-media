## What and why

The public site is built (Phases 3–13), but search engines and link previews see very little of it. Every route has a title and description, but there is no canonical URL, no Open Graph or Twitter card, no share image, no `sitemap.xml`, no `robots.txt`, and no structured data saying who RedHat Media is or where it works. When someone pastes a portfolio link into WhatsApp or LinkedIn today, it shows no image. That undercuts the mission's "the site is the proof" principle at the moment a prospect shares the work.

The Content Security Policy has run report-only since Phase 0. Only its anti-framing part is enforced. This phase **enforces the full policy** before the Milestone 1 release (Phase 16), after assessing whether `'unsafe-inline'` can leave `script-src`.

It also revisits the soft 404 (HTTP 200 + `noindex`) that unknown slugs get on `/services/[slug]` and `/portfolio/[slug]`.

## Out of scope

- Analytics, Speed Insights and their CSP needs (Phase 15). They load from the site's own origin (`/_vercel/…`), so they should need no CSP change.
- Turnstile in the CSP (Phase 18).
- A CSP reporting endpoint (`report-to`). It would be a public POST route that needs rate limiting and storage. Violations are caught by the e2e check instead (D11). Phase 15's alerting can revisit this.
- Search Console, sitemap submission and the custom domain (Phase 16).
- Location and industry landing pages (Phase 37).
- `preload` on HSTS (needs the custom domain; unchanged).

## Constraints

- **C1 — No new libraries.** `next/og` ships with Next. The font is an asset, not a dependency. `experimental.sri` (if adopted, D7) is a Next config flag.
- **C2 — Static stays static.** No route changes rendering mode: the build's route table is compared before and after (plan 4.1). The Open Graph image routes are prerendered where their page is.
- **C3 — No schema change.** No migration, no seed change.
- **C4 — CSP changes only with their test and `tech-stack.md`** (`CLAUDE.md`).
- **C5 — Existing suites hold.** `pnpm test`, `pnpm test:db` and `pnpm test:e2e` pass. Existing specs change only where the report-only header or the metadata shape is asserted.
- **C6 — Honest data.** Structured data and share cards state only facts already on the site: no ratings, reviews, prices, founding claims or invented addresses.
- **C7 — Performance budget.** No new client JavaScript. Share images are served from routes, not loaded by pages.
